The off-stage register. The bandage and the IV are the permanent condition, not a wound that heals.

"Travel 2.0" - Not an upgrade. Same stations, same signage, same everything. New version of me. The departure board at Union Station lists buses, trains, and metros in one column. Buses leave from both ends of the building. The metro is down a corridor on the left. The train is somewhere straight ahead, past a hallway that doesn't start labeling anything until you're nearly at the end of it. Every arrow on that board is correct. Not one of them answers the actual question, which is which of these three directions is mine. That board has always been like that. I have walked past it for years. In 1.0 you guess, you find out in ninety seconds, you correct, and by the time you sit down you've forgotten it happened. The ambiguity was always there. I just had the reserves to cover it. 2.0 has a hard energy cap and a walker with three bags on it.#losangeles #LAX #Travel #fyp #Ableism
